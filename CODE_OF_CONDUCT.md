# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, and disrespectful comments.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behaviour.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate corrective action in response to unacceptable behaviour.
